Our Story

Crafted with passion, served with purpose

Our Mission

At Lumina Café, we believe that coffee is more than just a beverage—it's a moment of connection, a pause in a busy day, and a celebration of craftsmanship. Since our founding in 2015, we've been committed to sourcing the finest single-origin beans and serving them with precision and care.

Our name, "Lumina," means light in Latin. We aim to bring light into our community through exceptional coffee, warm hospitality, and a welcoming space for everyone.

Our Space

Designed with warmth and intention, Lumina Café features exposed brick walls, soft ambient lighting, and comfortable seating arranged for both intimate conversations and focused work. We provide free high-speed WiFi and plenty of outlets for our work-from-coffee friends.

Our espresso bar sits at the heart of the café, allowing guests to watch our baristas work their magic. It's more than just a serving counter—it's a stage where coffee artistry comes to life.
